Verdict

I agree that the fable branch is the stronger replacement candidate, if the goal is a principled production direction for the no-intersection worm mechanics rather than a merge of two different physics proposals.

The reason is not that every open physics question is solved. The reason is that the fable branch makes narrower, more checkable claims and backs them with a stronger algebraic implementation: exhaustive elementary move generation, exact local charge-difference checks, explicit frozen-sector tests, and diagnostics for where the sampler actually moves. The comparison branch contains useful ideas, especially PlanarFluxUpdate, but those are different global tunneling physics and should stay experimental unless their role is demonstrated separately.

Why This Should Be a Replacement, Not a Merge

Different physical move classes

The comparison branch's PlanarFluxUpdate is a whole-lattice decomposable flux-sheet tunneling proposal. The fable branch's core contribution is a worm and string-worm program for moving through the constraint surface by local or semi-local coordinated changes. Those are not small variants of one algorithm; they probe and bias motion in different ways.

A merge would obscure interpretation

Mixing a global sheet update into the default hammer would make it harder to tell whether observed mobility comes from the worm catalog, wrapping loops, string dynamics, or rare sheet tunneling. If the fable branch is to be evaluated, its logic should be judged as a coherent stack.

Scientific Case

Systematic elementary move catalog

The branch replaces a hand-grown catalog with generated seeds from example/no-intersection-move-search.py. In the live tree, moves.py contains 41 orbit representatives: 39 three-link seeds and 2 two-link seeds. Expanding under signed axis permutations and re-anchoring produces 8 direction buckets with 828 shapes each, for 6624 elementary shapes.

This is the right kind of completeness claim: finite, reproducible, and tied to a stated search class.

Exact local charge algebra

The fable code factors the constraint check through Delta q = Delta F wedge F + F wedge Delta F + Delta F wedge Delta F and implements local_dq. The worm, constrained link update, and wrapping loop update use local charge differences instead of repeated global charge recomputation where possible.

Frozen-sector claims are tested directly

The branch contains explicit frozen configurations and tests showing that bounded elementary moves are blocked while torus-wrapping rings can remain legal. That is a stronger physics narrative than simply adding larger proposals: it identifies what is blocked, why it is blocked, and what kind of support gets around it.

StringWorm is a principled response to background dependence

The branch does not pretend the fixed elementary catalog exhausts all legal background-dependent motion. StringWorm addresses the fact that on nonzero F backgrounds, legal moves can be path-dependent and can require support that grows with the lattice. That is a more honest algorithmic split than continually expanding a fixed catalog.

What Not to Claim

Do not claim full ergodicity is proven

The branch gives a stronger move algebra and better evidence on known obstructions, but it does not prove global ergodicity of the complete no-intersection model.

Do not claim a universal three-link bound

The three-link bound applies to the current elementary empty-background unit-dipole catalog. In arbitrary backgrounds, legal head motion can include neutral loops or wrapping structures whose support grows with N.

Do not dismiss PlanarFluxUpdate as wrong

It is better described as orthogonal physics: a large global tunneling move that may be valuable as an explicit experimental kernel, not part of the fable replacement's default argument.

Do not overstate production validation

Targeted tests pass, but performance and autocorrelation comparisons across target couplings still need to be run before making a final production claim.

Suggested Replacement Pitch

Replace origin/feature/no-intersection-model with feature/fable-intersection-worm as the main no-intersection branch. The replacement is justified because it turns the worm update from an accumulating hand catalog into a generated, locally checked, test-backed move library; separates elementary worm motion from genuinely background-dependent string dynamics; and provides diagnostics for verifying whether the combined sampler moves everywhere it needs to move. Global planar sheet tunneling should remain a separate experimental kernel until its acceptance, autocorrelation impact, and physical role are measured.
